## The Problem

Most email platforms are expensive, complicated, and filled with features that small developers never use.

## Why Binboi Exists

We wanted a simple platform that lets developers send transactional emails without spending hours configuring infrastructure.

## Our Principles

- Simple APIs
- Transparent pricing
- Fast delivery
- Developer-first experience

## What's Next

We're building analytics, templates, webhooks, and more tools to help teams communicate with users reliably.
