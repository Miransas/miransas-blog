If you're a developer looking for your next role or freelance gig, a strong portfolio is non-negotiable. React makes it easy to build something fast, interactive, and memorable.

## What recruiters look for

- **Clean, working code** — link your GitHub
- **Live demos** — let people interact with your work
- **A clear narrative** — what problem did you solve?

## Choosing a template

### Match your goals

A portfolio for a design agency role should look different from one targeting backend positions.

### Keep it fast

Performance is a signal of craftsmanship. Optimize images and minimize JavaScript.

### Make it personal

Templates are a starting point. Add your voice, your projects, and your story.

## Conclusion

Your portfolio is a living project. Ship it, share it, and keep improving it as you grow.
