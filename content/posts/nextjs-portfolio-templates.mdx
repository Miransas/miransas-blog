A great portfolio is the single most effective way to land clients and jobs. With Next.js, you get blazing-fast performance, great SEO, and a developer experience that makes building one a joy.

## What makes a great portfolio?

A portfolio is more than a list of projects. It tells a story about who you are and what you can do.

- **Clear hero** — say who you are in one sentence
- **Selected work** — quality over quantity
- **Case studies** — show your process, not just the result
- **A way to reach you** — make contact effortless

## Template categories

### Minimal

Clean typography, lots of whitespace, and a focus on the work itself. Ideal for designers and writers.

### Interactive

Scroll animations, 3D scenes, and playful micro-interactions that demonstrate your front-end chops.

### Developer-focused

Code snippets, GitHub stats, and technical writing front and center.

## Conclusion

Pick a template that reflects your personality and the kind of work you want to attract. Then make it yours.
